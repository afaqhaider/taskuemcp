# Taskue for Claude

Taskue plans shifts, assigns tasks to staff and collects shift reports. This
plugin connects Claude to your Taskue company so administrators and owners can
review schedules, staff, tasks and reports, and create or update tasks and
one-time shifts.

## Connect

Install the plugin, open the Taskue connector and choose Connect. Sign in to
Taskue and approve access. Only active admin or owner accounts can authorize;
staff accounts are refused. You can revoke access at any time.

## Safety

- Every change is previewed first and applied only after you confirm.
- Nothing is permanently deleted; tasks and shifts are archived instead.
- Access is limited to your own company. Billing, accounts, submitted reports
  and recurring shifts are not changeable through the connector.
